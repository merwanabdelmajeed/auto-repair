Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Purrfect Auto Service ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our customer mobile app (the "App") to book and manage auto repair appointments.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information directly from you when you create an account and use the App:

  • Account information: first name, last name, and email address, used to create and secure your account.
  • Vehicle information: make, model, year, trim, VIN, license plate, and color for vehicles you add to your account, used to schedule and track service appointments.
  • Appointment information: requested services, scheduled dates/times, and appointment status/history.
  • Push notification token: a device-specific identifier used solely to deliver appointment status and promotional notifications to your device. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

We do not collect your precise device location, and we do not collect payment card information through the App (no in-app payment processing is currently offered).

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To schedule, track, and communicate about your service appointments
  • To send you appointment status updates and, if you opt in, promotional offers
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers that operate the App's underlying infrastructure (cloud hosting, authentication, and push notification delivery), solely to provide the App's functionality, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform those services on our behalf.

Data Retention

We retain your account, vehicle, and appointment information for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide you services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the information below.

Your Choices

  • You may update your name and email address from the App's Profile screen.
  • You may delete vehicles you've added at any time from the App's Vehicles screen.
  • You may disable push notifications at any time in your device's notification settings.
  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise any of the choices above, contact us at purrfect17auto@yahoo.com.
