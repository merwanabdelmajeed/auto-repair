Delete Your Account

Last updated: July 12, 2026

If you'd like to delete your Purrfect Auto Service account and the personal data associated with it, send a request to the email address below from the email address on your account.

Request account deletion: purrfect17auto@yahoo.com
Please include the phrase "Account Deletion Request" and the email address you registered with.

Deleting Individual Data (Without Deleting Your Account)

You don't need to delete your whole account to remove specific data. Vehicles you've added can be deleted at any time directly in the app: open the Vehicles screen, select the vehicle, and choose Delete. This removes that vehicle immediately — no email request needed.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account (name, email address)
  • Vehicles you've added to your account
  • Your appointment and service history

Processing Time

We will process your deletion request within 30 days of receiving it and confirm by email once it's complete. Some information may be retained longer where required for legitimate business or legal purposes (for example, financial records related to services already performed).

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at purrfect17auto@yahoo.com.
